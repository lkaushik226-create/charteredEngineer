Know Your Rights
& Requirements

MOEF · 20.01.2016

Export Repair — No NOC Required

No NOC for repaired electrical/electronic assemblies exported for repair and re-imported. However, CE certificate BEFORE export is essential to satisfy Customs.

Customs · Notif. 91/2009

Installation Certificate (EPCG / SFIS)

Customs Notification 91/2009 requires installation certificate from a CE confirming installation and use of imported capital goods within 6 months of import.

Companies Act · 2015

Useful Life of Assets — Depreciation Norms

Where companies use depreciation values different from Schedule II, variations must be certified by an independent technical expert.
